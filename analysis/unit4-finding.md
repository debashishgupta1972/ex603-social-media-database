# Unit 4 Finding

The platform recorded 200 reactions, and 12 were removed for spam. That gives a true platform-wide spam-removal failure rate of 6%. The rate for higher-volume posts, using posts with at least 10 events, was 5.67%. This is far below the roughly 50% failure rate that originally triggered concern.

Performance varies across posts. Post 2 had the highest failure rate among the higher-volume posts at 18.75%, followed by Post 3 at 15.38% and Post 1 at 9.09%. These posts should be reviewed first because their failure rates are higher than the overall baseline.

The results do not support treating the problem as a platform-wide failure. The better approach is to investigate the posts with unusually high failure rates while using the 6% platform rate as the overall reference point.
